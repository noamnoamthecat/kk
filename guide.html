💪 Fitness Guide

Everything you need to know to train smarter and eat better.

🏋️ Key Exercises

🦵

Squat

Builds quads, glutes, and core. The king of lower body exercises. Keep chest up, knees over toes.

🏗️

Deadlift

Full-body strength move targeting hamstrings, back, and grip. Keep the bar close and back neutral.

💪

Bench Press

Primary chest builder. Also works shoulders and triceps. Control the descent, press explosively.

🔼

Pull-Up

Best upper back and bicep exercise. Use full range of motion — dead hang to chin over bar.

🧍

Overhead Press

Builds strong shoulders and upper chest. Brace your core to avoid lower back strain.

🏃

Lunges

Great for single-leg strength and balance. Targets glutes, quads, and hip flexors.

😴 Recovery Tips

🌙

Sleep 7–9 Hours

Muscle is built during sleep, not in the gym. Prioritize sleep above everything else.

🧘

Stretch & Mobilize

10 minutes of stretching post-workout reduces soreness and improves range of motion.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Even mild dehydration reduces performance by up to 10%. Drink water throughout the day.

📅

Rest Days

Take 1–2 rest days per week. Active recovery (walks, yoga) is better than total rest.
